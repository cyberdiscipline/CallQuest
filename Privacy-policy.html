CallQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

CallQuest ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that counts calls and appointments you handle in your browser and turns them into XP, levels, goals and a companion character. This policy explains what it does with data.

What the extension reads

On Five9 pages, the extension looks at the page's on-screen call status (for example whether a call is active or has ended) so it can count calls. It does not read, record or store call audio, customer names, phone numbers, notes or any other call content.

What the extension stores

It stores only your own totals and settings: call and appointment counts and timestamps, your goals, level and XP, equipped gear, theme choices and window positions. If you use the optional protected actions (adding calls or resetting stats), a PIN you choose is stored on your device only, as a salted hash. It is never sent anywhere. This data is kept in Chrome's local storage on your device.

What is never collected or shared

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any server.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No account, sign-in or payment details are requested.

Exporting your data

The extension has an optional "Export CSV" and "Copy diagnostics" button. These create a file or clipboard text on your own device only. Nothing is uploaded.

Deleting your data

Use the reset option inside the extension, or remove the extension from Chrome, to erase everything it has stored.
